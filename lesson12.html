Act III: The Ideal (Sonnet 18)

这是莎士比亚最著名的十四行诗。他试图通过语言战胜时间。

💡 提示：按住 [空格键] 或鼠标长按诗歌查看现代翻译

任务： 找出这首诗中描述“大自然是不完美的 (Nature is imperfect)”的诗句。点击高亮至少两处。

Shall I compare thee to a summer's day? Thou art more lovely and more temperate: Rough winds do shake the darling buds of May, And summer's lease hath all too short a date; Sometime too hot the eye of heaven shines, And often is his gold complexion dimmed; And every fair from fair sometime declines, By chance or nature's changing course untrimmed; But thy eternal summer shall not fade, Nor lose possession of that fair thou ow'st;

Act IV: The Reality (Sonnet 130)

莎士比亚不仅会赞美，也会讽刺。这首诗是对传统情诗的“反讽 (Parody)”。

任务： 找出诗人“承认爱人并不完美 (Admitting flaws)”的句子。点击高亮至少两处。

My mistress' eyes are nothing like the sun; Coral is far more red than her lips' red; If snow be white, why then her breasts are dun; If hairs be wires, black wires grow on her head. I have seen roses damasked, red and white, But no such roses see I in her cheeks; And in some perfumes is there more delight Than in the breath that from my mistress reeks.

Final Act: The Playwright's Insight

🎭

你发现了莎士比亚的秘密。

Sonnet 18 使用了理想化 (Idealization)，通过贬低自然来赞美爱人。

Sonnet 130 使用了现实主义 (Realism)，通过承认缺陷来表达真爱。

这就是语言的影响力：它可以构建梦境，也可以打破幻象。

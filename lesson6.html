Unit 10.6: Confronting Anti-Intellectualism

Part 1: The Crumbling Pillar

我们正处在一个“反智主义（Anti-Intellectualism）”抬头的时代。正如 Ralph Waldo Emerson 所言，如果不加以维护，国家的智慧就会自我吞噬。

请填补这句名言缺失的单词，修复智慧的支柱：

"The mind of this country, taught to at objects, upon itself."

提示词：aim, eats, low

Part 2: Clearing the Fog (社论分析)

这是 Susan Jacoby 的文章 The Dumbing of America 的选段。目前它被“无知的迷雾”笼罩。

任务： 1. 鼠标悬停以驱散迷雾。
2. 找到作者的核心论点 (Claim) 并点击高亮它。
3. 找到作者提出的问题 (Problem) 并点击高亮它。

🖊️ Highlight Claim (论点) 🖊️ Highlight Problem (问题)
The mind of this country is indeed eating upon itself. It is not just that we are ignorant of facts. The problem is that we are increasingly proud of our ignorance. Anti-intellectualism is a virus that has mutated into a new, more dangerous form. It is the idea that "my ignorance is just as good as your knowledge."

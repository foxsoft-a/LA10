Task 01: Sentence Anatomy (语法解剖)

在写诗之前，我们先从散文开始。Scott Adams 说过一句关于影响力的话。

请选择 Subject (主语) 工具，然后点击句子中的主语部分。

You don't have to be a "person of influence" to be influential.

Task 03: The Blueprint (韵脚结构)

莎士比亚十四行诗遵循严格的结构：ABAB CDCD EFEF GG。

请将右侧的诗句（来自 Romeo and Juliet）拖入左侧正确的插槽，使其韵脚匹配。

If I profane with my unworthiest hand (hand)
To smooth that rough touch with a tender kiss. (kiss)
This holy shrine, the gentle fine is this: (this)
My lips, two blushing pilgrims, ready stand (stand)

UNIT 10.19 // MACBETH ACT 4

Step 1: Toxic Text Detector (历史回响)

邪恶并不总是面目狰狞，有时它伪装成“仁慈的政策”。

这是 Andrew Jackson 总统关于“印第安人迁移”的演讲。请将鼠标悬停在下划线词语上，查看其背后的残酷真相。

"It gives me pleasure to announce to Congress that the benevolent policy of the Government... in relation to the removal of the Indians... is approaching a happy consummation. It will separate the Indians from immediate contact with settlements of whites... and enable them to pursue happiness in their own way under their own rude institutions."

Step 2: The Impulse Circuit (Act 4.1)

Macbeth 在听到女巫的预言后，做出了一个可怕的决定。他说：

"The very firstlings of my heart shall be / The very firstlings of my hand."

任务： 以前他会思考（Head）。现在他切断了思考。点击 HEAD 节点将其短路，然后点击 ACTION 连接 Heart 与 Hand。

HEART
(Impulse)

(Reason)

(Action)

Step 3: The Archetype Scale (原型天平)

Macbeth 是一个悲剧英雄（值得同情但有缺陷）还是一个纯粹的恶棍？

将下列成分拖入你认为最合适的盘中。这决定了你 Essay 的立场。
